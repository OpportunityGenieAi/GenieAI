Fully funded scholarships for international students

A fully funded scholarship pays for the main costs of studying abroad. Here is what that usually means, how to spot a genuine offer, and how to keep track of deadlines.

What "fully funded" usually covers

  • Tuition fees for the whole programme.
  • A monthly stipend or living allowance.
  • Travel support, often a return flight.
  • Health insurance in some programmes.
  • Visa or settling-in support in some programmes.
The exact package changes from one programme to the next. Always read the official call to see what is included, because "fully funded" is not a legal term and some programmes use it loosely.

Where genuine fully funded offers come from

  • Government programmes such as Chevening, Fulbright, DAAD and Türkiye Bursları.
  • European programmes such as Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters.
  • University-level awards from institutions in Canada, Sweden, Switzerland, South Korea and more.
  • Foundations and development banks that support students from specific regions.
Start with the official website of the funder or the university. Aggregator sites are useful for discovery, but the application rules on the official page always win.

How to avoid scholarship scams

  • A real scholarship never asks you to pay to be selected.
  • Check that the link leads to the funder or university domain.
  • Be careful with offers that arrive by message and promise guaranteed money.
  • Compare the details with the official page before sending documents.

A simple routine that works

  • Pick three to five programmes that match your level and country.
  • Write each deadline down with a reminder two to three weeks earlier.
  • Collect transcripts, a CV and referee contacts before the call opens.
  • Draft your motivation letter once, then adapt it for each programme.

Frequently asked questions

What does fully funded mean?

It normally means tuition plus a living allowance, and often travel costs. The exact coverage is set by each programme, so check the official call.

Can I apply for more than one fully funded scholarship?

Yes. Most students apply to several programmes at once, as long as each one allows it.

Do I need to pay to apply?

Genuine scholarships do not charge to select you. Some universities charge a normal admission application fee, which is separate.
