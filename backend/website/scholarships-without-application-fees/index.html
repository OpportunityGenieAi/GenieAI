Scholarships and applications without application fees

Application fees add up fast when you apply to several programmes. Many scholarships are free to enter, and many universities offer fee waivers.

Scholarship fee versus admission fee

  • A scholarship application is usually free.
  • A university admission application may carry a fee, even when the scholarship does not.
  • Some countries, such as Germany and Sweden, use central application portals with different fee rules.
Always confirm which fee you are being asked for before you pay anything.

Ways to avoid or reduce fees

  • Look for universities that state "no application fee" on their admissions page.
  • Ask for a fee waiver if you are applying from a low-income country or have financial need.
  • Apply through scholarship programmes that handle the application themselves.
  • Check whether your embassy or a partner organisation covers the fee.

Never pay to be considered

  • Genuine funders do not charge for selection.
  • Do not pay "processing" or "guarantee" fees to third parties.

Frequently asked questions

Are scholarship applications free?

Most are. The university admission application is a separate step and may have a fee.

How do I ask for a fee waiver?

Write to the admissions office, explain your situation briefly and attach any proof they list on their website.
