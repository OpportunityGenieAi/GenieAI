Master's scholarships for international students

Funding for a master's degree comes from governments, universities and foundations. Start early, because many deadlines are a year before the course starts.

Where master's funding comes from

  • Government schemes such as Chevening, Fulbright, DAAD and Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships.
  • Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Degrees.
  • University merit awards in countries like Sweden, Denmark, Canada and Taiwan.
  • Foundation and company scholarships.

Documents most programmes ask for

  • Transcripts and degree certificate.
  • A motivation letter or statement of purpose.
  • Two reference letters.
  • A current CV.
  • Proof of English, unless waived.

Timeline

  • 12 months before: shortlist programmes and check the language rules.
  • 6 to 9 months before: request references and draft your letter.
  • Final 8 weeks: complete forms and upload documents.
Dates vary by programme. Use the official call for exact dates.

Frequently asked questions

When should I start applying?

Start at least 9 to 12 months before the course begins.

Can I get a master's scholarship with a low GPA?

Some programmes weigh work experience, leadership and research more than grades. Read the selection criteria for each one.
