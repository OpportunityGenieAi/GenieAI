Scholarships for African students

Several large programmes are open to students from across Africa. This guide lists well-known options and how to prepare a competitive application.

Programmes to look at first

  • Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program through its partner universities.
  • Mandela Rhodes Foundation Scholarship.
  • Canon Collins Trust scholarships for Southern Africa.
  • Beit Trust scholarships.
  • Pan-African University scholarships.
  • DAAD in-country and in-region programmes.
  • Government scholarships from Türkiye, Hungary, China, South Korea and others.
Eligibility depends on your country, level and field. Check the official page for each programme.

Make your application stronger

  • Choose programmes that match your level and your country.
  • Show leadership and community impact with specific examples.
  • Ask referees early and give them your CV and the scholarship criteria.
  • Keep your documents scanned and named clearly.

Stay organised

  • Deadlines for many programmes fall between September and March.
  • Create a list with one row per scholarship and update it weekly.

Frequently asked questions

Which scholarships accept students from my country?

Each programme sets its own list of eligible countries. Check the eligibility section of the official call.

Can I apply while still in my final year?

Many programmes accept final-year students with a predicted result. Read each call.
