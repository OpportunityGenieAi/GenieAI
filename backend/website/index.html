Questions about the app

What is OpportunityGenie AI?

It is a free Android app that lists scholarships, fellowships and other study-abroad opportunities with official links and deadlines, plus an AI Advisor that gives feedback on your application writing.

Is the app free?

You can install the app from Google Play and browse scholarships. Check the Play Store listing for the latest details on any paid features.

How do I find fully funded scholarships?

Open the app, search or filter by region, and read the funding and deadline details. Always confirm on the official page before applying.

Can the AI Advisor write my motivation letter?

It helps you improve your own writing with suggestions. Your application should always be your own work.

Is there an iPhone version?

The app is currently available on Google Play for Android.
