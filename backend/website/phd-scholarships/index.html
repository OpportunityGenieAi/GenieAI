PhD scholarships and funded doctoral positions

Many PhD places come with funding attached. The route differs by country: some advertise positions, others expect you to find a supervisor first.

Two main routes

  • Advertised positions: a university or project posts a funded role with a fixed deadline.
  • Scholarship plus supervisor: you find a supervisor, agree a research plan, then apply for a scholarship such as DAAD, Chinese Government, Türkiye Bursları or a university award.

Approaching a supervisor

  • Read two or three of their recent papers.
  • Write a short email with your background, your idea and why you chose them.
  • Attach your CV and keep the message under 200 words.

What to prepare

  • A research proposal.
  • Academic CV and transcripts.
  • References from lecturers who know your research.

Frequently asked questions

Is a PhD usually paid?

In many countries, yes, through a scholarship or a salaried position. Check the funding details of each offer.

Do I need a research proposal?

Most programmes ask for one or for a short research statement.
