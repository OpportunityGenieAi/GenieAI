Privacy Policy

OpportunityGenie AI

Effective date: September 19, 2026 · Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information OpportunityGenie AI ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") collects, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. OpportunityGenie AI helps students find, evaluate, and track scholarship and academic opportunities, with tools such as GPA conversion, scholarship matching, an AI advisor, application tracking, and notifications about new scholarships. The App is intended for adults and university-level students (18 and older) and is not directed at children.

By using OpportunityGenie AI, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you sign up with email, we collect your name, email address, and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are stored only as a secure one-way hash. We never see or store your actual password. We also store your preferred language setting. A small number of older accounts created before we moved to our current authentication provider may have a password hash stored in our own database.

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google (see Section 4). We do not receive your Google password.

Academic & profile information

Information you choose to enter to get personalized results, which may include your GPA and grading system, academic level, field of study, nationality, English test scores (such as IELTS), years of work experience, publications, leadership and volunteering experience, and whether you have supporting documents ready (CV, statement of purpose, recommendation letters). We do not collect the documents themselves.

Scholarship tracking data

Records of scholarships you save or track, along with your self-reported application status (for example saved, applied, interview, accepted, rejected).

AI advisor conversations

The messages you type to the AI advisor, and the replies you receive. To personalize answers, relevant information from your profile may be included with your message. See Section 5.

Push notification information

If you allow notifications, we collect a push notification token (a device-specific identifier issued by a push notification service), your device platform (Android), and whether notifications are enabled for that device. The token is linked to your account so we can send you notifications. See Section 3.

Payment information

If you subscribe to a premium tier, the purchase is made and paid for through Google Play (which may use Google Pay or another payment method saved in your Google account). We do not collect or store your card details or other payment credentials. We receive and keep purchase information from Google Play, such as your purchase token, order ID, product, and subscription status and renewal or expiry dates, so we can give you access to premium features.

Advertising & device information

We display ads through Google AdMob to support the free tier. AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers (such as your advertising ID), general device information, IP address, and approximate location to serve and measure ads. See Section 6.

Automatically collected technical information

Like most apps, we automatically receive standard technical information when you use the App, such as device type, operating system version, app version, IP address, and general usage and crash logs. These are used to keep the App secure and reliable. The App also checks for app updates from an update service, which receives basic device and app-version information for that purpose.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, SMS, or precise location.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and verify your identity, including sending confirmation and password-reset codes by email
  • To provide core features: GPA conversion, scholarship matching, the AI advisor, and application tracking
  • To send push notifications about new scholarships (see below)
  • To process subscription payments and manage your subscription tier
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the App, including diagnosing technical issues and preventing abuse
  • To display advertising that supports the free tier
  • To communicate with you about your account or important changes to the App
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Push notifications

With your permission, the App sends push notifications when a new scholarship is added.

  • What is sent: the scholarship's name, provider, country, and level. If you have a saved profile, the notification may also show how well the scholarship matches your profile (for example "82% match"). Matching is done on our servers using your profile. Users whose profile fits a scholarship poorly are not notified.
  • How it is delivered: notifications travel through a push notification delivery service and Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), which deliver them to your Android device. These services receive your push token and the notification content.
  • Your control: you can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings (Settings > Apps > OpportunityGenie AI > Notifications), or by declining the permission prompt. Logging out of the App also stops notifications to that device. If a token becomes invalid, we delete it automatically.

Notifications are not used for advertising.

4. Sign in with Google

You can create an account or log in using your Google account. When you do, Google shares your name and email address with us. Google tells us only what you authorize on its sign-in screen. We use this information to create and identify your account, in the same way as an email sign-up. Google processes your data under Google's Privacy Policy. You can review or remove the App's access in your Google Account settings under "Security > Your connections to third-party apps and services."

5. AI advisor

The AI advisor answers questions about scholarships and applications. When you send a message, we pass its text, and relevant profile details used to personalize the answer, to third-party AI providers so it can generate a reply. These providers process the data to produce the response under their own terms and privacy policies. Please do not enter sensitive personal information (such as passport numbers, financial account details, or health information) in advisor chats. AI-generated answers can be wrong, so check important details, such as deadlines and eligibility, on the scholarship's official website.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial ads. AdMob may collect and process data, including your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and approximate location, to serve ads and measure their performance. This may include personalized and non-personalized advertising, depending on your device settings and region. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads.

You can control ad personalization in your device settings (for example, by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads). See Google's Partner Sites policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Payments & subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold and billed through Google Play Billing. Google, not us, collects and processes your payment details, handles billing, renewals, refunds, and cancellation, and decides which payment methods are available (including Google Pay). We never receive or store your full card number, CVC, or bank details. Google's practices govern the payment data it collects. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Payments Privacy Notice. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in the Google Play Store under "Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions."

8. Service providers

We use trusted third-party service providers to run the App. They process data on our behalf, only as needed to provide their services. These include Google (sign-in, push notification delivery, advertising through AdMob, and subscription billing through Google Play), an authentication and email delivery provider, a push notification and app update delivery service, third-party AI providers that generate advisor replies, and a cloud hosting provider for our servers and database. Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy, in addition to this one.

9. How we share information

We share information only with the service providers described in this policy, who help us run the App and are not permitted to use it for their own unrelated purposes (except as described for AdMob in Section 6). We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights, safety, or security of our users or the App, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case we will tell you before your data becomes subject to a different privacy policy. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers.

10. Data storage & security

Your account and profile data is stored on secure servers hosted with a reputable cloud hosting provider. Data is transmitted between the App and our servers over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Passwords are stored only as irreversible hashes, and your login session token is kept in your device's secure storage. Access to our administration tools is limited to authorized administrators.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International data transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States and the European Union. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards offered by those providers for such transfers.

12. Data retention

We keep your account and profile information while your account is active, or as needed to provide the App's features. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your associated personal data within 30 days of verifying your request, except where we must keep certain records (such as payment and transaction records) for legal or accounting reasons. Push tokens are deleted when they become invalid or when your account is deleted. See How to delete your account.

13. Your rights & choices

  • Access & correction: you can review and update most of your profile information in the App.
  • Deletion: you can request deletion of your account and data at any time. See Section 14.
  • Notifications: you can turn push notifications off in your device settings at any time.
  • Ad personalization: you can limit ad tracking in your device's system settings.
  • Google sign-in: you can revoke the App's access in your Google Account.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under data protection laws (such as the GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA, or Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019), including the right to access a copy of your data, to data portability, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

14. How to delete your account

You can ask OpportunityGenie AI to delete your account and the data linked to it at any time. Anyone can make this request, including people who have already uninstalled the App.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to info@opportunitygenie.org from the email address registered to your account.
  2. Use the subject line: Account deletion request.
  3. We will confirm your request, verify that it comes from the account owner, and delete your account within 30 days.

Data we delete

  • Your account details: name, email address, password hash, and your sign-in record with our authentication provider
  • Your academic and profile information
  • Your saved scholarships and application tracking records
  • Your AI advisor conversation history stored on our servers, if any
  • Your push notification tokens
  • Your subscription status and the link between your account and your Google Play purchase records (purchase token and order ID)

Data we may keep

  • Purchase and transaction records that we or Google must retain for legal, tax, or accounting reasons, for the period required by law
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Please cancel it in the Google Play Store first so you are not billed again.
  • Anonymized usage and diagnostic data that cannot identify you

You can also ask us to delete only some of your data, without closing your account, by emailing the same address and telling us what to remove. This information is also available on its own at opportunitygenie.org/delete-account.html.

15. Children's privacy

OpportunityGenie AI is intended for adults and university-level students (18 years of age or older) and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.
