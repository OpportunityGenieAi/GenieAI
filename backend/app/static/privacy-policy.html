Privacy Policy

OpportunityGenie AI

Effective date: September 19, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information OpportunityGenie AI ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. OpportunityGenie AI helps students find, evaluate, and track scholarship and academic opportunities, including GPA conversion tools and application tracking. This app is intended for use by adults and university-level students (18 and older) and is not directed at children.

By using OpportunityGenie AI, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a password (stored only as a secure one-way hash — we never store or can see your actual password). We also collect a security question and answer for account recovery purposes.

Academic & profile information

Information you choose to enter to get personalized results, which may include your GPA and grading system, academic level, field of study, nationality, English test scores (e.g. IELTS), years of work experience, publications, leadership and volunteering experience, and whether you have supporting documents ready (CV, statement of purpose, recommendation letters).

Scholarship tracking data

Records of scholarships you save or track within the app, along with your self-reported application status (e.g. saved, applied, interview, accepted, rejected).

Payment information

If you subscribe to a premium tier, payments are processed by Stripe, our payment processor. We do not collect or store your full card details — Stripe collects this directly. We retain a Stripe customer reference ID and your subscription tier status.

Advertising & device information

We display ads through Google AdMob to support the free tier of the app. AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers (such as your advertising ID), general device information, and approximate location to serve and measure ads. See Section 3 for details.

Automatically collected technical information

Like most apps, we automatically receive standard technical information when you use OpportunityGenie AI, such as device type, operating system version, app version, and general usage/crash logs, used to keep the app running reliably.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account, and verify your identity for account recovery
  • To provide core features: GPA conversion, scholarship matching, and application tracking
  • To process subscription payments and manage your subscription tier
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the app, including diagnosing technical issues
  • To display advertising that supports the free tier of the app
  • To communicate with you about your account or important changes to the app
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

OpportunityGenie AI uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial ads. Google AdMob may collect and process data — including your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and general location — to serve ads and measure their performance. This may include both personalized and non-personalized advertising, depending on your device settings and region.

You can control ad personalization through your device's own privacy settings (for example, resetting or limiting your advertising ID in your phone's system settings). For details on how Google handles this data, see Google's Partner Sites policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Payments & subscriptions

Subscription payments are handled entirely by Stripe, a PCI-compliant third-party payment processor. We never receive or store your full card number, CVC, or bank details. Stripe's own privacy practices govern the payment data it collects — see Stripe's Privacy Policy.

6. Data storage & security

Your account and profile data is stored on secure servers hosted through Render. Data is transmitted between the app and our servers using encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Passwords are never stored in plain text — only as irreversible cryptographic hashes.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data retention

We retain your account and profile information for as long as your account remains active, or as needed to provide you the app's features. If you delete your account, we delete your associated personal data within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain certain records (e.g. payment records) for legal or accounting purposes.

8. Your rights & choices

  • Access & correction — You can review and update most of your profile information directly within the app.
  • Deletion — You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us (see below).
  • Ad personalization — You can limit ad tracking through your device's system settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable data protection laws (such as the GDPR or CCPA), including the right to data portability or to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

9. Children's privacy

OpportunityGenie AI is intended for use by adults and university-level students (18 years of age or older) and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you within the app. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
