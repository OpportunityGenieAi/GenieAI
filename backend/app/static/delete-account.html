Account deletion

Delete your OpportunityGenie AI account

This page explains how to request deletion of your OpportunityGenie AI account and the data linked to it. You can make this request even if you have uninstalled the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to info@opportunitygenie.org from the email address registered to your account.
  2. Use the subject line: Account deletion request.
  3. We will confirm your request, verify that it comes from the account owner, and delete your account within 30 days.

Data we delete

  • Your account details: name, email address, password hash, and security question and answer
  • Your academic and profile information
  • Your saved scholarships and application tracking records
  • Your subscription tier status and the link between your account and your Stripe customer ID

Data we may keep

  • Payment and transaction records that we or Stripe must retain for legal, tax, or accounting reasons, for the period required by law
  • Anonymized usage and diagnostic data that cannot identify you

Delete only some of your data

If you want specific information removed without closing your account, email the same address and tell us what to delete.
